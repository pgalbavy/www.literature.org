Welcome! Explore classic fiction and non-fiction by authors including [Lewis Carroll](/authors/carroll-lewis/), the Brontë sisters ([Anne](/authors/bronte-anne/), [Charlotte](/authors/bronte-charlotte/), and [Emily](/authors/bronte-emily/)), [Jack London](/authors/london-jack/), [Mark Twain](/authors/twain-mark/), [Charles Dickens](/authors/dickens-charles/), [Charles Darwin](/authors/darwin-charles/), and [Rene Descartes](/authors/descartes-rene/). Browse the <a class="home-authors-list-link" href="/authors/"><em>Authors list</em></a> and find a book to enjoy at your own pace.

We are refreshing the collection and improving the reading experience over the coming months. Thank you for visiting; we hope you find something you love, and we [welcome your suggestions](/contact/).

<p class="home-features-note">We've recently made reading more comfortable. Books now open in page mode: turn pages with the arrow keys, a swipe, a tap (or double-click with a mouse) in the margins, or the arrows at the top, and wide screens can show two pages side by side. Prefer scrolling? Open Reading settings in the menu to switch, and to adjust the page width, font, text size, or theme. If enabled, we'll remember your last reading paragraph and take you back there when you visit the home page from outside literature.org. The share icon in the menu shares any page, or the exact paragraph you're reading in a book, and the Authors list is now sorted by surname with a quick A–Z index. We hope you enjoy them!</p>

<p class="home-privacy-note">Privacy note: This site is served from static files and does not use cookies or collect personal data. Reader settings and your last reading location stay in this browser and are never sent to literature.org. The hosting provider may keep standard web traffic logs that we cannot access. JavaScript is required for the site's navigation and reading features.</p>
