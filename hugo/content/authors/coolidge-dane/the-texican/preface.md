"Oh, out from old Missouri I set me forth to roam Indicted by a jury For toling hawgs from home.

"With faithful Buck and Crowder I crossed the Western plains Then turned them loose in the Cow-Country And waited for my gains.

"And now I'm called a Cattle King With herds on many a stream— And all from the natural increase Of that faithful old ox-team." The Song of Good-Eye.
