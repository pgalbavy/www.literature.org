THE ARGUS PHEASANT Ah, God, for a man with a heart, head, hand, Like some of the simple great ones gone Forever and ever by; One still, strong man in a blatant land, Whatever they call him--what care I?-- Aristocrat, democrat, autocrat--one Who can rule and dare not lie! Tennyson.
