# Changes to the collection

What has been added to, removed from, or corrected in the published works, newest first. Add an entry whenever a book is published, withdrawn or its text is changed. Links are site paths.

## 28 November 2026

### Corrected chapter addresses

- [*The Head of the House of Coombe*](/authors/hodgson-burnett-frances/the-head-of-the-house-of-coobe/) by Frances HODGSON BURNETT: the 32 chapters were published under *The Good Wolf*'s address (`/authors/hodgson-burnett-frances/the-good-wolf/the-head-of-the-house-of-coobe/`), so their page titles, breadcrumbs and Information panel named the wrong book. They now sit under the book's own address; the old addresses no longer work.

## 27 November 2026

### Restored chapters

Each of these books had chapter text missing since its original conversion, mostly because the Project Gutenberg source repeats or misprints a chapter number, which made the converter write two chapters to the same page. The missing text was taken from fresh downloads of the same Gutenberg ebooks (from the official mirror, gutenberg.pglaf.org, while the main site was down for maintenance) and formatted to match the surrounding chapters. The newer Gutenberg editions also correct a few words and restore some italics in the restored chapters.

- [*A Room With A View*](/authors/forster-e-m/a-room-with-a-view/) by E. M. FORSTER (Gutenberg #2641): restored Part 2, Chapter 9, *Lucy As A Work Of Art*. A sentence in Chapter 15 ("Chapter two was found, and she glanced at its opening sentences.") had been taken for a chapter heading, so the rest of Chapter 15 was showing as a separate, misplaced Chapter 9; it is now back at the end of Chapter 15.
- [*The Turmoil*](/authors/tarkington-booth/the-turmoil/) by Booth TARKINGTON (Gutenberg #1098): restored Chapter 21. The source numbers Chapter 31 as "XXI", so Chapter 31 had replaced it; Chapter 31 is now at its own page.
- [*The Guest of Quesnay*](/authors/tarkington-booth/the-guest-of-quesnay/) by Booth TARKINGTON (Gutenberg #5756): restored the final chapter, Chapter 23, which the source also numbers "XXII".
- [*The Young Scout*](/authors/ellis-edward-sylvester/the-young-scout/) by Edward Sylvester ELLIS (Gutenberg #57699): restored Chapter 17, *The Shadow Of Danger*, and Chapter 18, *A Cruel Blow*. The source numbers Chapters 19 and 20 as "XVII" and "XVIII"; Chapters 19 to 29 are now numbered correctly, so the pages for chapters 17 to 27 show different text than before.
- [*Prince Otto*](/authors/stevenson-robert-louis/prince-otto/) by Robert Louis STEVENSON: checked; nothing was missing (*Prince Errant* is the title of Book 1, not a chapter).
