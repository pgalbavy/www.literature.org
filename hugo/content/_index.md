On this site you will find classic works of English literature. Fiction from writers like Lewis Carroll, the Bronte sisters, Jack London, Mark Twain, and Charles Dickens, plus non-fiction from Charles Darwin and Rene Descartes.

For those unfamiliar with copyright law, we are unable to make works available that are not in the public domain.
