Welcome to literature.org

Welcome! Explore classic fiction and non-fiction by authors including Lewis Carroll, the Brontë sisters (Anne, Charlotte, and Emily), Jack London, Mark Twain, Charles Dickens, Charles Darwin, and Rene Descartes. Browse the Authors list and enjoy reading at your own pace.

We will be updating the collection and improving the reading experience over the coming months. Thank you for visiting; we hope you find something you love, and we welcome your suggestions.

Start exploring

We focus on works in the public domain, so some more recent titles cannot be offered. You can contact us with questions or suggestions.

Privacy note: This site is served from static files and does not use cookies or collect personal data. Reader settings stay in this browser and are never sent to literature.org. The hosting provider may keep standard web traffic logs that we cannot access. JavaScript is required for the site's navigation and reading features.
